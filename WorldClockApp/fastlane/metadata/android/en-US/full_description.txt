ChronoSphere is an offline world clock for keeping track of the places that matter to you.

• Add and save cities across the Americas, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Oceania.
• Search the included time zone list and keep as many places as you need.
• See each city's current local time, day, date, and time zone.
• Remove places at any time; your list stays on this device.
• Add the home screen widget to see up to five of your saved cities.
• Tap the widget to open the app. The widget refreshes periodically and when you edit your list.
• No account, network connection, ads, or tracking.

ChronoSphere uses Android time zone data, including daylight saving time changes. Your saved list never leaves this device.
