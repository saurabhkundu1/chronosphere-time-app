Applify World Clock is a beautiful, real-time world clock application that displays the current time in 5 major cities around the globe: New York, London, Tokyo, Sydney, and Dubai.

Key Features:
• Real-time clock updates every second
• Displays 5 global cities with their respective time zones
• Beautiful card-based UI design
• Home screen widget for quick access
• Material Design 3 theme
• No ads, no tracking - completely privacy-focused
• Open source and free

The app provides an intuitive interface showing each city's name, current time, and date. The home screen widget allows you to check world times without opening the app.

Perfect for travelers, remote workers, or anyone with friends and family across different time zones.
